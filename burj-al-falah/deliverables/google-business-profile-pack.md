## First: eligibility, before anything is created

Your registered address is **Warehouse 4, Al Sajaa St 129, Al Sajaa Industrial, Sharjah**.
That needs care, because Google's rules distinguish sharply between storefronts and
service-area businesses.

Google's current guidance:

- Businesses with storefronts must display the address and maintain *"permanent fixed
  signage of their business name at the address."*
- Service-area businesses — those who *"serve customers at client locations rather than a
  storefront"* — should *"hide your business address from customers."*
- *"P.O. boxes or mailboxes located at remote locations aren't acceptable."*
- A virtual office *"isn't eligible for a Business Profile"* unless staffed during
  business hours.
- Service area *"shouldn't extend farther than about 2 hours of driving time from where
  your business is based."*

### Three questions decide your setup

1. **Does the warehouse have permanent signage with your business name on it?**
2. **Do customers actually visit it during business hours?**
3. **Is it staffed during business hours?**

**If customers don't visit** — which is typical for a joinery workshop — then you are a
**service-area business**: create the profile at the warehouse, then **hide the address**
and set Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman as your service area. This is the correct, compliant
setup, and Google explicitly recommends hiding the address in this case. It is not a
downgrade.

**If the warehouse is signed, staffed and does receive clients**, you may show the
address. Only choose this if all three are genuinely true.

Your service areas are all within two hours of Al Sajaa, so the distance limit is fine.

**Never** create a second profile, use a home or virtual address, or invent a location.
Duplicate or fake listings get suspended, and recovering a suspended profile is slow.

## Photos — the part most businesses get wrong

Photos drive more profile engagement than text. You are in a strong position: your site
carries 19 projects of real, well-shot work.

**Suggested opening set (10–15):**
- **Logo** and a **cover photo** — your best completed interior
- **6–8 completed projects** — kitchen, bathroom, reception, TV unit, stair, vanity
- **2–3 work-in-progress or workshop shots** — these build trust disproportionately;
  people want to see real joinery being made
- **1–2 team at work** (with their permission)

**Rules:** your own photographs only. Never stock imagery. No added logos, borders or
text overlays — Google's guidelines prefer unedited images. Confirm client permission
before publishing any interior of a private home.

⚠️ **The "Burj Khalifa Apartment Bathroom Furniture" project needs explicit confirmation**
before it appears anywhere public — both that the work was genuinely done there, and that

## After it exists

1. **Verify it.** Usually postcard, phone or video. Unverified profiles show little.
2. **Add hours**, including Ramadan and public-holiday variations.
3. **Post occasionally** — a completed project every few weeks. Not daily; nobody is
   watching, and it's time you don't have.
4. **Answer questions** in the Q&A section. Anyone can answer there, including people
   who don't know your business. Seed the genuine common ones yourself.
5. **Reviews** — see `reviews-workflow.md`. This is the single biggest lever available.
6. **Check the insights monthly** — searches, views, calls, direction requests, website
   clicks. These feed the monthly report.

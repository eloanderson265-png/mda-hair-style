## 6. Conflicts to resolve

1. **Sector conflict.** The original brief described a *fit-out company*; the domain's
   history is a *garments trading* business; the owner now states *technical services*.
   Whether Garments Trading and Technical Services are the same legal entity,
   a rebrand, or two separate companies sharing a domain is unresolved.
2. **Emirate conflict.** Facebook result says Dubai; Instagram result says Sharjah;
   the former garments shop was in Rolla, Sharjah. Google Business Profile category
   and location pages depend on the answer.
3. **Unrelated business in the same folder.** This repository also contains
   `index.html` for *MDA Hair Style*, a ladies' salon in Sharjah — a different business.
   It has not been modified.
4. **Name ambiguity risk.** Several UAE firms use near-identical names
   (Bab Al Falah Technical Services, Burj Al Arab Technical Services, Burj Al Minaal,
   Burj Al Najah, AM Al Falah Group). Exact legal name and licence number matter for
   correct identification in local search.

# Case Study Template — turning 19 projects into evidence

You already have 19 project pages with real photography. That's a genuine asset and most
competitors don't have it. What they lack is **detail** — currently a title and images.

Specifics are what persuade a buyer weighing up contractors, and what gives a search
engine or AI assistant something concrete to cite. "Custom TV Unit" is a label.
"A 3.2m oak-veneer media wall for an apartment in Al Nahda, fitted in four days" is
evidence.

---

## What I need per project

For each, roughly this. Approximations are fine — **but never invent one.**

| Field | Example | Publishable? |
|---|---|---|
| Property type | 2-bedroom apartment | Usually |
| Area / district | Al Nahda, Sharjah | Usually — general area, never a full address |
| Client name | — | **Only with written permission.** "A private client" is fine |
| Scope | Remove existing units, fabricate and install 4.2m of wardrobes | Yes |
| Materials | Oak veneer, soft-close hinges, SPC flooring | Yes — buyers care |
| Size | 4.2 linear metres / 85 sq m | Yes |
| Duration | Six working days on site | Yes — one of the most-asked questions |
| Year | 2025 | Yes |
| A challenge and how it was solved | Uneven wall required a scribed filler panel | Yes — this is the most persuasive part |
| Cost | — | **Your call.** A range ("AED 18,000–24,000 for work of this type") is useful. Exact client figures are confidential |
| Photo permission | Confirmed with client | **Required before publishing** |

## Structure for each page

```markdown
# [What it was] — [Property type] in [Area]

[One or two sentences: what the client needed and why.]

**At a glance**
- Property: [type]
- Location: [district, emirate]
- Scope: [summary]
- Materials: [key ones]
- Duration: [x days/weeks on site]
- Completed: [year]

## What the space needed
[The starting point. What was wrong, missing or dated.]

## What we did
[The work, in order. Removal, fabrication, installation, finishing.]

## The tricky part
[A real constraint and how it was handled. Do not skip this — it is the section
that convinces people you have done this before.]

## The result
[What changed for the client.]

[Photos: before · in progress · completed]
```

## An honest note on "before" photos

Your site has a `/before-after/` section — good, it's persuasive. But only use genuine
pairs of the same space. Never pair a bad stock "before" with your real "after".

---

## Priority order

Don't do all 19 at once. Start with the five representing work you most want more of:

1. **Kitchen Solution** — high-value, high-intent searches
2. **Bathroom Renovation** — same
3. **Reception Solution** — your commercial credential
4. **Custom TV Unit** — most-searched joinery item
5. **Office Kitchen** — commercial again

The rest can follow at one a week.

## Two flags before publishing

⚠️ **"Burj Khalifa Apartment Bathroom Furniture"** — confirm the work was genuinely done
there and that naming the building publicly is permitted. Landmark buildings often carry
confidentiality terms. If unconfirmed, retitle to something accurate and neutral.

⚠️ **"Nursery Landscape — Abu Dhabi"** sits outside your published service area of Dubai,
Sharjah and Ajman. Either widen the stated area or note that the project was outside your
normal coverage.

**Send me the details for any project, in any rough form — voice note, bullet points,
WhatsApp message — and I'll write it up.**
